What We Offer

Comprehensive training and development across every stage of growth

From K-12 career exploration to professional development and business coaching, Mass2Miami delivers culturally sensitive, results-driven programs tailored to your organization's unique needs.

Youth & Education Programs

Alternative career pathways and immersive learning experiences designed for K-12 students and school districts.

Our premier professional development series for nonprofit leaders, staff, and board members. Monthly workshops covering essential topics in organizational management, fundraising, strategic planning, governance, and mission-driven leadership.

"Teacher for a Day" Program

8-week intensive program teaching students how to start a business, understand financial fundamentals, and develop entrepreneurial thinking.

"Nurse for a Day" Program

K-8 exploration of healthcare career pathways and professional roles in the nursing and health sectors.

Academic Services

One-on-one and small group tutoring in mathematics, reading, writing, science, and technology for grades 2-8. All sessions facilitated and supervised by certified teachers.

Academic Enrollment Workshops

Comprehensive preparation for academic success including ACT/SAT preparation, MCAS/EOC test readiness, FASFA guidance, scholarship workshops, and financial literacy for students and parents.

Professional Development Training

Customized training modules in small business development, leadership, entrepreneurship, social responsibility, diversity & inclusion, and cultural responsiveness.
