Key Achievements

Milestones of Community Leadership

Mass2Miami has been recognized by government agencies, nonprofit partners, and educational institutions for our commitment to excellence and community impact. Here are some of our significant milestones.

01

Massachusetts Rehabilitation Commission Partnership

Selected as a key partner in a $900,000 state initiative to address employment needs of young adults with disabilities in the Springfield area. Focused on childcare and food service occupational training programs.

02

BBB Accreditation

Better Business Bureau accreditation demonstrating our commitment to ethical business practices, transparency, and customer satisfaction in all consulting and training relationships.

03

The Amador Foundation

Philanthropic giving focused on youth development, reading, math, STEM, the arts, diversity, and cultural awareness. Demonstrates our commitment to giving back and supporting community sustainability.

04

School District Partnerships

Career exploration and professional development programs integrated into K-12 schools and districts across Massachusetts and Florida. "Entrepreneur for a Day," "Nurse for a Day," and "Teacher for a Day" programs reaching thousands of students.

05

Community Learning Laboratories

Established and supported over 20 community learning labs in partnership with schools, nonprofits, and civic associations. These spaces serve as hubs for youth exploration, professional development, and economic education.

06

Specialized Workforce Training

Provided occupational training in childcare, food service, healthcare, and entrepreneurship to hundreds of participants. Measured outcomes show 70%+ employment placement rates and participant satisfaction scores of 4.5+/5.0.
