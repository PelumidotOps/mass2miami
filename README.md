## 🌟 About MASS2MIAMI

**MASS2MIAMI LLC** is a mission-driven leadership consultancy and education group dedicated to developing confident leaders, stronger organizations, and thriving communities. We partner closely with school districts, non-profit organizations, institutions, and community enterprises across Massachusetts and Florida to design and operate transformative **Community Learning Laboratories**.

Led by President & CEO **Gilberto Amador, M.Ed., Ed.S.**, Mass2Miami brings over 35 years of executive, academic, and supervisory leadership experience. Our programs combine proven organizational practices with deep respect for local culture, lived experience, and sustainable community impact.

---

## 🎯 What We Do

### 1. Professional Development & Organizational Leadership
Comprehensive training programs and curriculum in organizational leadership, digital-age literacy, strategic governance, and critical thinking skills for executive directors, staff, and non-profit board members.

### 2. Entrepreneurship & Economic Education
Instructional tutoring, economic development curriculum, and community learning labs built alongside partner institutions. We guide participants through financial fundamentals, business model design, and economic self-sufficiency.

### 3. Youth & Career Exploration Programs
Culturally responsive programs that foster teamwork, social-emotional learning, and career awareness:
- **"Teacher for a Day" Program**: An 8-week intensive introducing students to education, leadership, and public speaking.
- **"Nurse for a Day" Program**: K–8 immersive healthcare career exploration opening pathways into nursing, medicine, and allied health fields.
- **Academic Tutoring & Test Readiness**: Grade 2–8 academic tutoring led by certified educators, plus ACT/SAT prep, MCAS/EOC readiness, and FAFSA guidance.

### 4. Small Business Coaching
Hands-on coaching designed to help entrepreneurs understand the ins and outs of their operations, streamline strategy, and build lasting organizational growth.

### 5. The Academy
A monthly workshop series held on the third Tuesday of each month covering essential non-profit and executive leadership competencies:
- Executive Strategy & Change Management
- Non-Profit Board Governance & Compliance
- Fundraising & Donor Cultivation
- Community Learning Lab Intensive
- Cultural Responsiveness & Multicultural Leadership

## 💻 Local Development

### Prerequisites
- Node.js (v18 or higher)

### Run Locally
```bash
# Start the local development server
npm run dev

# Open http://localhost:3000 in your browser
```

### Production Build
```bash
# Generate the production static bundle
npm run build
```
Creates a distribution-ready bundle in the `dist/` directory.
